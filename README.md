# Excel-Data-Analyst-project
Project Title: Consumer Complaints Analysis Dashboard
Overview

The Consumer Complaints Dashboard is designed to analyze and monitor customer grievances reported against various financial service providers. The dataset typically includes complaints related to products such as credit cards, loans, mortgages, banking services, and credit reporting agencies.
This dashboard helps stakeholders understand customer pain points, identify trends over time, and evaluate how effectively complaints are being resolved.

By transforming raw complaint records into interactive visual insights, this dashboard supports data-driven decision-making and contributes to improving customer satisfaction and service quality.

Objectives

To identify common complaint categories and understand which products/services generate the most dissatisfaction.

To analyze company performance in handling customer complaints.

To understand consumer issues geographically across states or regions.

To monitor resolution status (resolved, in progress, rejected, etc.).

To support organizations in improving customer service strategies.

Scope

This project focuses on:

Collecting and cleaning the complaints dataset.

Categorizing complaints based on product type, issue, location, and company.

Visualizing the complaint distribution across geography and time.

Evaluating company response and resolution performance.

Providing a user-friendly interactive dashboard for exploration.

It does not include automated prediction or complaint classification using machine learning (can be done as future enhancement).

Dataset Description

Typical columns included:

Column	Description
Date Received	When the complaint was submitted
Product / Service	The financial product involved (loan, banking, cards, etc.)
Issue / Sub-Issue	The nature of the complaint
Company	The company against which complaint was filed
State / Region	Consumer’s location
Submitted Via	Web, Phone, Email, Referral, etc.
Company Response	Explanation or resolution action
Resolution Status	Whether complaint was resolved or still pending
Key Insights (Example Findings)

Credit reporting and credit card services generate the highest number of complaints.

A few large companies receive a significantly higher share of complaints compared to smaller firms.

Most complaints originate from densely populated states, typically urban regions.

While many complaints are resolved on time, some categories show delayed or unsatisfactory responses.

Certain products show recurring patterns, indicating systemic issues in service quality.

Dashboard Explanation

Your dashboard likely contains the following major sections:

1. KPI Summary Cards

Displays key metrics such as:

Total Complaints Received

% Resolved vs Pending

Number of Companies Involved
These give a quick high-level snapshot of the dataset.

2. Complaints by Product / Service (Bar Chart)

Shows which financial products generate the most complaints.
This helps identify problem-prone service areas.

3. Complaints by Company (Bar or Pareto Chart)

Ranks companies based on complaint volume.
Helps identify industry leaders vs laggards in customer satisfaction.

4. Complaints by State (Map or Horizontal Bar Chart)

Visualizes geographic concentration of complaints.
Useful for regional performance improvements.

5. Resolution Status Distribution (Pie or Donut Chart)

Shows how complaints are being handled:

Resolved

In Progress

Closed without explanation
Evaluates company responsiveness.

6. Trend Over Time (Line Chart)

Displays complaint volume across months/years.
Used to track seasonal patterns or improvement efforts.

Conclusion

The Consumer Complaints Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer dissatisfaction trends across financial service products, companies, and geographic regions. By presenting insights visually, the dashboard helps stakeholders identify the root causes of complaints and evaluate the effectiveness of company responses.
This analysis supports better policy design, service improvements, and consumer protection strategies.
